## Garden club

The Sunflower Garden Club meets on the first Saturday of every month at 9am.

## Recipe: lemon rice

Lemon rice needs 2 cups of cooked rice, the juice of 1 lemon, mustard seeds, curry leaves and peanuts.
Fry the mustard seeds and curry leaves first, then add the peanuts, then the rice and lemon juice.
It serves 3 people.
