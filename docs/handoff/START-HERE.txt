MIKO for Windows - version {{VERSION}}
======================================

1. Double-click  {{INSTALLER}}

   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info",
   then "Run anyway". (MIKO isn't signed by a big company yet, so
   Windows doesn't recognise it. This is expected.)

2. Wait for the green bar to finish. You don't need to click anything.
   MIKO opens by itself when it's done.

3. Follow the short "Meet MIKO" steps. You can skip any of them.
   To open MIKO later: Start menu -> MIKO.

Hide / show MIKO any time: Ctrl + Shift + R
Talk to MIKO: hold Shift + A, speak, let go.
See your open apps: hold Tab, press Q.
Help: right-click the small MIKO icon near the clock -> "Help & Setup".
